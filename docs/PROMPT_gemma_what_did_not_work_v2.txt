We conducted these experiments on earlier versions of the method using the ten MLSP languages unless otherwise specified. Each difference is reported relative to the version of the method used at that time.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Eye-tracking dataset.} We tested a version of our method for Romanian alone that included eye-tracking measures from 95 readers in MultiplEYE \citep{jakobi-etal-2025-multipleye}. These measures exist for 95\% of the Romanian targets. They raised the correlation by 0.007, with a 95\% interval of $-0.001$ to 0.014, which includes zero. This is an upper bound because their weight was chosen on the test scores. The recordings cover only these Romanian texts. We also tried to predict the total reading time from the XLM-R base embedding. This embedding exists in every language. The embedding alone predicted the total reading time worse than word length and frequency did: 0.517 against 0.626. The embedding together with word length and frequency gave 0.622.
\item \textbf{LLM surprisal.} We tested the surprisal of the target in its context as a third signal from a language model. With Qwen3 8B, it lowered the fusion by less than 0.001 and made Filipino worse. With the average of six language models, it lowered the fusion by 0.001. German required a positive weight for surprisal. Filipino required a negative weight.
\item \textbf{Fine-tuning XLM-R.} In an earlier setup without LOLO, we trained on the English CompLex data and tested on 2{,}827 MLSP 2024 instances. The best of 14 runs fine-tuning the whole encoder reached 0.461. This was against 0.551 with the encoder frozen. The best of six LoRA runs reached 0.462. This was against 0.515.
\item \textbf{Few-shot prompt.} We wrote a prompt with six labelled examples from the trial split of the other languages. On the trial split, it was our best single prompt with 0.697. On the test split, it lowered the fusion by 0.012 as the only prompt and made five languages worse. As a fourth prompt next to our three prompts, it changed the fusion by less than 0.001.
\end{itemize}
